# Keys.so AI tracker (GEO)

`keysso_ai_tracker(report=...)`: `projects` (no tracker_id) lists trackers. With `tracker_id`: `entity`
(brand, systems, groups), `dates`, `report` (prompt mentions for date_from..date_to), `mentions`,
`citation` (site cited in sources), `reference` (brand vs competitors visibility), `sources`
(view=url|domain), `competitors`, `prompt` (answers for prompt_id on date).

`systems` IDs: 1 GigaChat, 3 DeepSeek, 5 Perplexity, 6 ChatGPT, 8 Alice AI in Search, 9 Google AI Mode,
10 Gemini, 11 Claude, 12 Alice AI chat. Read-only: never creates, starts or deletes trackers.
